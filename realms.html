Siege & Vassalage

Players

Pass & play on one device.

How to play

  • Your army: Queen on your corner, Rook, Bishop, Knight and 4 Pawns around her. Your King waits in the palace outside the grid and only watches.
  • Roll 1–4: move one piece. Queen, Rook and Bishop slide up to that many squares, the Knight makes its L-jump (over walls too), Pawns go 1 or 2 squares forward and capture diagonally.
  • Roll 5 💣 Bomb: smash any wall segment, or blow up an enemy piece sitting in a Haven.
  • Roll 6 🛡 Haven: make one of your pieces immune to normal attacks until it moves.
  • Walls: each realm is walled off. Only the 2-square gates let pieces through (Knights jump).
  • Storm the palace: land on an enemy's corner gate square and that kingdom falls.
  • Promotion: a Pawn reaching one of the 3 gold-marked squares beside an enemy palace becomes a Queen, Rook, Bishop or Knight.
  • Surrender: demand it from a weakened player (3 pieces or fewer). If they accept, their army turns into your Pawns.
  • Win by being the last kingdom standing.
